What it is产品介绍

Interpreted phone calls电话口译

The caller dials one number and picks a language. The system then calls the other person and interprets in both directions with AI speech-to-text, translation and text-to-speech. The person on the other end needs no app.主叫拨一个号码并选择语言，系统再拨给对方，在两人之间用 AI 做双向语音识别、翻译和语音播报。对方不用装任何 App。

Third-party call assist协助翻译

Already on a call with someone? Add an assist line to the call so a translator joins and both sides can be understood.已经在和对方通话？把协助线加进通话，由翻译接入，让双方都能听懂。

Face to face (web)面对面翻译（网页）

Two people open a web page on their own phones, scan a code and hold to talk. No app to install. Supports a 24-language picker.两个人各用手机浏览器打开网页，扫码加入，按住说话，不用安装 App。支持 24 种语言选择。

How it was built技术概要

  • Twilio Voice with Media Streams for two-way call audio.Twilio Voice + Media Streams 处理双向通话音频。
  • Speech-to-text, translation and text-to-speech using OpenAI, Deepgram, DeepL and ElevenLabs.语音识别、翻译和语音合成使用 OpenAI、Deepgram、DeepL、ElevenLabs。
  • One Node.js service with a prepaid wallet and ledger (SQLite) and a web front end.一个 Node.js 服务，带预付钱包与账本（SQLite）和网页前端。
